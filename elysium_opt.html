A presentation about my dream island

ELYSIUM

A small island where the sea is warm, the evenings are slow, and everything I love fits within a bike ride.

Sasu Luca · Clasa a XI-a A

Chapter 1 · Where & what

Small enough to cycle around

Location

Elysium lies in a warm, imaginary sea near the tropics, far away from big cities and busy shipping routes.

Size

It is only about 12 km from end to end, so you can cycle all the way around it in one afternoon.

Landscape

A sleeping volcano covered in forest rises in the middle, surrounded by white sand and a calm lagoon in the south.

Getting there

There is no airport. You arrive by sailboat or by a small seaplane that lands right on the lagoon.

Chapter 2 · Climate & nature

Summer that never really ends

Climate

It is around 27 °C all year, so it always feels like summer. Every afternoon a cool breeze comes in from the sea, and when it rains, it is a short, warm shower that is over in minutes. There is no winter at all.

Plants

Tall coconut palms line the beaches, and the gardens are full of mango, banana and papaya trees. Higher up, the cloud forest is always misty, with giant ferns, wild orchids and hidden waterfalls.

Animals

Sea turtles come back every year to lay their eggs on the beach, and dolphins often play in the bay. The coral reef is home to thousands of colourful fish, and parrots call in the forest all day.

Chapter 3 · Places

Four places I'd build

Place 4 of 4 · The north hill

Hilltop Observatory

Because there are no big cities nearby, the night sky over Elysium is completely dark and full of stars. On the highest hill there is a small observatory with a big telescope that anyone can use. On clear nights you can see planets, the Milky Way and even shooting stars.

No light pollutionTelescope for everyone

Place 3 of 4 · The east cape

Coral Reef Dive Center

Just off the east cape lies a colourful coral reef, and the dive center is the gateway to it. Anyone can learn to snorkel or dive here, and a glass-bottom boat takes out visitors who'd rather stay dry. Under the water you can swim next to turtles, rays and thousands of bright fish.

Snorkel & dive lessonsGlass-bottom boat

Place 1 of 4 · The south lagoon

Lagoon Village

This is where everybody lives. The houses stand on wooden stilts above the calm, turquoise lagoon, and each one is painted a different colour. Instead of streets there are little bridges, so you can walk from your front door straight onto a boat, or jump in for a swim.

Houses on stiltsBridges instead of streets

Place 2 of 4 · The volcano

Volcano Hot Springs

The volcano in the middle of the island has been asleep for thousands of years, but it still warms the water underground. On its slopes there are natural rock pools that stay hot all year. After a long day, I'd sit in the warm water and watch the sunset.

Warm pools all yearBest at sunset

Chapter 4 · A day on Elysium

From sunrise to starlight

07:00

Sunrise swim

I'd start the day with a swim in the warm lagoon as the sun comes up, then have breakfast on the dock: mango, coconut and pancakes.

10:00

Into the forest

In the morning I'd hike up the cloud forest trail with my friends, cross the rope bridge and cool off under the waterfall.

18:30

Sunset dinner

After a lazy afternoon in a hammock, everyone meets on Sunset Beach to grill fish, play music and watch the sky turn orange.

22:00

Stars & hot springs

The day ends in the warm volcano pools, and then it's up the hill to the observatory to look for shooting stars.

Chapter 5 · How life works

Calm, clean and together

Topic 1 of 4

Energy

All of the electricity on Elysium comes from the sun and the wind. Every roof has solar panels, and three wind turbines stand on the windy north cape. Batteries store the extra energy, so the lights stay on at night. Nothing is burned, so the air is always fresh and the sea stays clean.

Solar on every roofNo petrol, no smoke

Topic 2 of 4

Getting around

There are no cars on the island, and nobody misses them. People ride bikes along shady paths, and small electric carts carry heavy things. A little open-air tram goes around the coast every 20 minutes, and kayaks are the fastest way across the lagoon.

Bike paths everywhereCoastal tram

Topic 3 of 4

Food

Most of the food grows right on the island. There are gardens full of mangoes, bananas and coconuts, and a shared farm where everyone helps one morning a week. Fishermen only catch what the island needs, so the reef never runs out of fish, and leftovers go into the compost.

A shared farmZero food waste

Topic 4 of 4

Community

Only a few hundred people live on Elysium, so everybody knows each other. Once a month there is a big festival on the beach with music, dancing and food from every family. There is one rule everyone agrees on: protect the reef and the turtles.

Monthly beach festivalRule no. 1: protect the reef

Chapter 6 · A message in a bottle

My must-have list

A treehouse home

My house would be a big treehouse in the palm trees, with a rope ladder, a hammock on the balcony and a sea view from my bed.

An underwater room

Under the dock there would be a room with glass walls, so I could watch fish and turtles swim past while I read.

My own sailboat

With a small sailboat I could explore the coast, find secret beaches and visit the other islands nearby whenever I want.

Family & friends

The most important thing would be the people I love. Even a perfect place isn't perfect if you are there alone.

Thank you for visiting

See you on Elysium

Elysium is my idea of a perfect place: warm sea, wild nature, clean energy and the people I love, all on one small island. We can't live there yet, but we can bring a little of it into our real lives. Any questions?
